# Task 2: Data Visualization and Storytelling

## Objective

The objective of this task is to create meaningful visualizations that communicate business insights and tell a compelling story using sales data. The dashboard was developed using Power BI and the Superstore dataset.

## Tools Used

* Power BI Desktop
* Superstore Dataset

## Dashboard Components

### 1. Sales by Category

A bar chart showing total sales across different product categories.

### 2. Profit by Category

A column chart comparing profits generated by each category.

### 3. Sales by Region

A bar chart displaying sales performance across different regions.

### 4. Sales Trend by Year

A line chart illustrating sales growth over time.

### 5. Top Products by Sales

A chart highlighting the top-performing products based on sales.

### 6. Region Slicer

An interactive filter allowing users to analyze sales by region.

## Key Insights

* Technology generated the highest sales and profit among all categories.
* Office Supplies contributed moderate sales and profit.
* Furniture generated comparatively lower profit.
* The West region recorded the highest sales performance.
* Sales increased significantly from 2015 to 2017, indicating business growth.
* A small number of products contributed a large share of total revenue.

## Data Storytelling

The dashboard provides a clear overview of business performance by highlighting sales trends, regional contributions, category profitability, and top-selling products. It enables stakeholders to identify growth opportunities, understand customer demand patterns, and make informed business decisions.

## Best Practices Followed

* Used appropriate chart types for different analyses.
* Maintained a clean and uncluttered dashboard layout.
* Applied consistent formatting across visuals.
* Highlighted important business insights.
* Included interactive filtering for better user exploration.
