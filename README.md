# 📊 Task 2: Data Visualization and Storytelling

## 🎯 Objective

Create meaningful visualizations that communicate business insights and tell a compelling story using sales data. The dashboard was developed using Power BI and the Superstore dataset.

---

## 🛠️ Tools Used

* Power BI Desktop
* Superstore Dataset
* GitHub

---

## 📈 Dashboard Overview

The dashboard provides an interactive view of sales performance, profitability, regional contributions, and product-level insights.

### Dashboard Components

✅ Sales by Category

✅ Profit by Category

✅ Sales by Region

✅ Sales Trend by Year

✅ Top Products by Sales

✅ Region Slicer (Interactive Filter)

---

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

### 💰 Category Performance

* Technology generated the highest sales and profit.
* Office Supplies contributed moderate revenue and profit.
* Furniture generated comparatively lower profit.

### 🌍 Regional Analysis

* West region recorded the highest sales.
* East region followed closely behind.
* South region contributed the least sales.

### 📅 Sales Trend

* Sales increased significantly from 2015 to 2017.
* Business growth remained consistent over the years.

### 🏆 Product Performance

* A small number of products generated a large share of total sales.
* Top-selling products can be targeted for future promotions and inventory planning.

---

## 📖 Data Storytelling

This dashboard transforms raw sales data into actionable business insights.

By combining trend analysis, regional performance, category profitability, and top-product analysis, decision-makers can:

* Identify high-performing categories
* Understand regional sales distribution
* Monitor business growth over time
* Focus on products driving maximum revenue
* Make data-driven business decisions
